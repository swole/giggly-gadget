# Fixture: a messy Inbox recipe, the way they arrive

Miso ginger salmon w/ bok choy (weeknight)
serves 2, about 25 min total (10 min prep, 12 min cooking)

2 salmon fillets (skin on, ~150g each)
1 block firm tofu (300g) (optional, for extra protein)
2 tbsp white miso
1 tbsp mirin
1-2 tsp grated ginger
2 cloves garlic, minced
300g bok choy (halved)
1 tsp sesame oil
salt to taste

Mix the miso, mirin, ginger and garlic into a paste. Rub it over the salmon and rest 10 min.
Pan-sear skin-side down for 4 min, flip, 3 min more. Steam the bok choy 3 min and toss with the sesame oil.
Serve the salmon on the bok choy with the tofu on the side if using.
